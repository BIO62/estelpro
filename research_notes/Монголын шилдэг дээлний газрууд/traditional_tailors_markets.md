# Traditional / Master Deel Tailors, Custom Deel Workshops, and Deel Markets in Mongolia (as of Nov 2026)

> **Method caveat (read first):** In this research session, direct page fetching (WebFetch) was blocked for every Mongolian domain tried (ikon.mn, unuudur.mn, mn.wikipedia.org) and for meanwhileinmongolia.com (DNS not allowed in the sandbox). Every finding below comes from **web search result snippets/summaries**, not from reading full articles. Treat the details (especially phone numbers, floors, prices) as **leads that need checking**, not verified facts. Most sources are 1 to 8 years old. No source confirmed 2026 opening hours or 2026 prices for any specific tailor.

## 1. Who are the recognized master deel makers / workshops, and what is their reputation?

### Takeaway
I found no published, authoritative list of "best deel masters." The named, documented makers are a mix of (a) individual artisans profiled in the press (e.g., Uriankhai deel maker B.Erdenechuluun of Khovd, who holds the "Соёлын тэргүүний ажилтан" title), and (b) established Ulaanbaatar workshops/brands (Ур Ундрах ХХК, Moncouture, Tod Torgo, Michel & Amazonka / Deel Brand, Сондор, Ивээл, Tuko tailor). The annual "Дээлтэй Монгол" festival names a "best deel maker" (шилдэг дээл урлаач) each year, but I could not find winners' names.

### Cited Findings
**Individual masters / artisans**
- **Б.Эрдэнэчулуун (B.Erdenechuluun), Khovd aimag, Uriankhai ethnic deel specialist**: in 2014 she made over 100 Uriankhai costume and felt-craft works by herself and was awarded the **"Соёлын тэргүүний ажилтан"** title; she learned from her seamstress mother; demand grew after she set up a cultural heritage center in Мөнххайрхан soum; she reports about **1.5 million MNT/month** income from deel making and takes orders **online from Erdenet, Ulaanbaatar, Bayan-Ölgii and Khovd** — [Montsame](https://www.montsame.mn/mn/read/300587). (Article is roughly 4 years old according to the search summary; status in 2026 unverified.)
- **Ж.Маамуу**, a 90-year-old deel seamstress with about 70 years' experience; says she can now make a deel in one day and used to make three per day when young — [Eguur.mn](https://eguur.mn/571884/). (No location or contact given in the snippet.)
- **З.Цэнгэлсайхан**, deel/daalin sewing teacher; says more than half her students came to "sew a daalin for their husbands" — [NIO.mn](https://www.nio.mn/5614/).
- **А.Доржханд (A.Dorjkhand)**, national-costume researcher with her own studio (opened about 6 years before the interview); her long/short jackets that keep core deel elements are popular, with orders from abroad — [Uzeg.info 2251](https://uzeg.info/i/2251); [Uzeg.info 2189](https://www.uzeg.info/i/2189).
- **С.Саран (S.Saran)**, artist who makes felt deels with no stitching (sculptural felting); her felt deel won **1st place at the 2024 national avant-garde costume competition** — [Eguur.mn](https://eguur.mn/507852/).
- **О.Болд (O.Bold)**, researcher of Xiongnu-era clothing; received a top young designer award and shared the **Grand Prix (costume design) at the "Гоёл" competition, 2003** — [fashion.coo.mn](https://fashion.coo.mn/%D0%9C%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%BB/page10/&thisy=&thism=&thisd=).
- **Ц.Минжмаа**, senior seamstress; profile "Ээжийн минь уран ухаан намайг оёдолчин болгосон" — [Ergelt.mn](https://ergelt.mn/news/35/single/6351).
- **А.Алтантуяа** (profiled on the Ур Ундрах company site) — [ur-undrakh.com](http://www.ur-undrakh.com/?choose=294&ext=more).
- State award example (garment industry, not deel-specific): veteran seamstress **Т.Цэрэндумаа** of the "Тэс-Эрдэнэ" sewing factory received the "Аж үйлдвэрийн гавьяат ажилтан" title — [President's Office](https://president.mn/22748/).

**Festival and heritage context**
- **"Дээлтэй Монгол" наадам** (Deel Festival) is held each July at Sükhbaatar Square; the 19th edition was 8 July 2025 at 11:00; awards include best district, best organization, **best deel maker (шилдэг дээл урлаач)**, and best-dressed family — [Montsame](https://www.montsame.mn/mn/read/373337); [UBN](https://ubn.mn/p/86655). The 20th edition (2026) also picked the best deel maker, best-dressed couple and best-dressed foreigners — [MNB](https://news.mnb.mn/i/348934). Organized by the Capital City Tourism Department with the Ministry of Culture, the Mongolian Tourism Association and the **"Монгол костюмс" center** — [Montsame](https://www.montsame.mn/mn/read/347521).
- **UNESCO:** the deel is **not** on UNESCO's ICH Representative or Urgent Safeguarding lists. "Mongolian deel culture" appears only as a 2010 entry in UNESCO's multimedia archive, and claims that the deel became Chinese intangible heritage are false — [MFCC fact-check](https://mfcc.mn/factcheck-1250/); Mongolia's 13 listed ICH elements — [Shuud.mn](https://www.shuud.mn/a/490831), [Gogo.mn](https://gogo.mn/r/wdqkk). The national register is held by the Center of Cultural Heritage — [ncch.gov.mn](https://ncch.gov.mn/ich?ItemID=4).
- **"Соёлын тэргүүний ажилтан" rules:** at least 5 years in the culture sector plus contribution/achievement criteria; awarded by the Minister after a majority vote of the award council; recipients are published on the ministry website — [Montsame](https://montsame.mn/mn/read/252703). Fake versions of the badge have been sold in Erenhot (China), so check the ministry's list — [Ugluu.mn](https://ugluu.mn/109301.html).

### Inferences
- "Master" status in deel making is mostly built on reputation (press profiles, festival prizes, word of mouth) rather than a formal registry. The only deel maker I could confirm holds "Соёлын тэргүүний ажилтан" is B.Erdenechuluun (Khovd, Uriankhai).
- Since the deel itself is not a UNESCO-listed element, there is no such thing as a "UNESCO-recognized deel craftsperson." Any marketing that claims this should be treated with caution.
- "Монгол костюмс" center (co-organizer of the Deel Festival) and Ур Ундрах look like the most institutionally established options in Ulaanbaatar.

## 3. Best marketplaces / centers to order a custom deel; prices; turnaround

### Takeaway
**Narantuul (the "Black Market")** has the biggest and cheapest selection of ready-made deels and fabrics. The **State Department Store** (a national clothing/deel section, reportedly on the 5th floor) is central and convenient for tourists but pricier. Boutique and custom workshops (Moncouture, Tod Torgo, Michel & Amazonka, Ур Ундрах) charge more. Custom orders take about 2–3 weeks at boutiques; local tailors can do it in a few days. Holiday-season prices rise.

### Cited Findings
- **Narantuul**: "cheapest and biggest selection" of deels — [Meanwhile in Mongolia](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/where-to-buy-mongolian-deel/) (snippet); less polished than malls, pickpocket warnings — [Discover Mongolia (2026 guide)](https://www.discovermongolia.mn/blogs/shopping-in-mongolia).
- **Narantuul prices**: the popular styles sell for **230–250k MNT**; lamb-lined deels **2.5–3.5 million MNT**; silver-fitted belts **30–120k MNT**; headline "Нарантуул захад дээл 250 мянгаас 3.5 сая төгрөг" (date not shown in the snippet) — [Zindaa](https://news.zindaa.mn/4hxx). Cashmere deels **750k–1.6M MNT** (Tsagaan Sar report); Narantuul is cheaper than other malls — [Arslan.mn](https://www.arslan.mn/lifestyle/advice/599648.shtml).
- Older price points: Naadam deel **120–150k MNT** — [Ikon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/1m7t); graduation (Хонхны баяр) deel orders **from 100k MNT** — [News.mn](https://news.mn/r/2548316/); a report that deel demand, sewing fees and retail prices have risen — [Unuudur](https://www.unuudur.mn/a/242386); Naadam deels **250k–3M MNT** (search summary) — [Montsame](https://www.montsame.mn/mn/read/347521).
- **Narantuul relocation risk**: in 2025 the city announced plans to move Narantuul and Да хүрээ markets to the ends of the city's expressways to ease congestion, and land clearance had begun — [iSee.mn](https://isee.mn/n/73846). **Check where Narantuul is located in 2026.**
- **State Department Store**: Discover Mongolia calls it the best all-round first stop for visitors — [Discover Mongolia](https://www.discovermongolia.mn/blogs/shopping-in-mongolia); its 5th floor is "stylish and trendy but pricey" for deels — [Meanwhile in Mongolia](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/where-to-buy-mongolian-deel/) (snippet). Narantuul and the State Department Store sell ready-made deels, and a tailor can make one to measure in a few days — [Guru Travel Mongolia](https://tour2mongolia.com/travel-tips/traditional-clothes-deel) (search summary). Note: one search found no source explicitly confirming a national-clothing floor in the Mongolian-language Wikipedia floor list, so treat "5th floor" as one blogger's claim — [mn.wikipedia](https://mn.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%A3%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B1%D0%B0%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%80_%D0%98%D1%85_%D0%94%D1%8D%D0%BB%D0%B3%D2%AF%D2%AF%D1%80).
- **Custom turnaround**: Moncouture 14–21 days — [Meanwhile in Mongolia](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/custom-mongolian-clothing/); a 90-year-old master can make one in a day — [Eguur.mn](https://eguur.mn/571884/).
- **Guided custom-deel experience for foreigners**: choose fabric at **Sunday Plaza**, then a local seamstress sews it; **$300 USD per person, fabric not included** — [Meanwhile in Mongolia](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/custom-mongolian-clothing/) (snippet).
- How a professional workshop splits the work: fabric/felt preparation, sewing, a fitter for measurements, specialists for buttons, embroidery and decoration, and a technologist overseeing it — [Montsame](https://www.montsame.mn/mn/read/300587) (search summary).
- Rental (for photo shoots or events): Mongol Kino rental lists deels — [rental.mongol-kino.mn](https://rental.mongol-kino.mn/deel-262).

### Inferences
- Rough price tiers, combined from older sources (likely higher in 2026 because of inflation): plain/Naadam deel ~120–250k MNT; mid-range festive deel ~250k–750k; cashmere ~0.75–1.6M; lamb/fur-lined or luxury wedding deel ~2.5–3.5M+.
- Order 3+ weeks before Naadam (July), Tsagaan Sar (Jan/Feb) and school graduation (Хонхны баяр, May–June). Demand peaks then, and tailors report multiple orders before national holidays — [Eguur.mn](https://eguur.mn/571884/).
