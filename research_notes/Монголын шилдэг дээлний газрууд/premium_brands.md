## Which deel makers/brands are most frequently cited as the best (awards, festivals, media, celebrity/state clients, reviews)?

### Takeaway
The names with the strongest evidence are **Michel & Amazonka** (international press, three Olympic uniform commissions) and **Mongol Costumes / Монгол Костюмс** (makes state and Naadam-ceremony deels, co-organises the "Дээлтэй Монгол" festival). Most other candidates in the brief (Torgo, Tsagaan Alt, Goyol, Zuuni Deel, Mongol Deel House) either turned out to be something else or had no evidence behind them.

### Cited Findings

**Michel & Amazonka (Michel&Amazonka): the most-cited designer label with a deel base**
- Founded by sisters Choigaalaa Yanjindulam (Michel) and Nyamkhand (Amazonka). Sources disagree on the year: 2013 per ikon.mn/Wikipedia-style pages, 2015 per designboom/Time. — [ikon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/2ac1); [designboom](https://www.designboom.com/design/team-mongolia-uniforms-paris-2024-olympics-games-parade-deel-michelamazonka-07-19-2024/)
- Designed Team Mongolia's Paris 2024 opening/closing ceremony uniforms, based on the deel: embroidered vests over long tunics and gold-thread collars. The uniforms went viral worldwide (Time, Dazed, Highsnobiety, Vogue HK). — [Time](https://time.com/7003999/paris-olympics-uniforms-designers-mongolia); [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/fashion/article/63163/1/designers-behind-team-mongolia-viral-olympics-2024-kit-michel-amazonka-paris); [Highsnobiety](https://highsnobiety.com/p/mongolia-olympic-uniforms)
- Earlier made Team Mongolia's uniforms for Tokyo 2020 and Beijing 2022. Each Paris uniform was tailored to the athlete's measurements and took about 20 hours. CEO is sibling Munkhjargal Choigaalaa. — [designboom](https://www.designboom.com/design/team-mongolia-uniforms-paris-2024-olympics-games-parade-deel-michelamazonka-07-19-2024/); [Options the Edge](https://www.optionstheedge.com/node/4015)
- The uniforms drew criticism at home alongside praise abroad. — [Global Voices](https://globalvoices.org/2024/07/19/mongolias-olympic-uniforms-draw-praise-abroad-and-criticism-at-home/)
- Was picked from 10 designers in the national team ceremonial/sport uniform selection. — [ikon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/2ac1)
- A ceremonial deel by the brand was reportedly donated to the Olympic Museum in Lausanne. — [Wikipedia mirror](https://wiki.riteme.site/wiki/Michel_%26_Amazonka) (secondary; not verified at the source)
- Forbes Brasil (2019) called it "the most successful fashion brand in Mongolia". A travel guide calls it "the hottest store in Ulaanbaatar for statement deels". — [Forbes Brasil](https://forbes.com.br/negocios/2019/09/conheca-as-irmas-da-michelamazonka-a-marca-fashion-mais-bem-sucedida-da-mongolia/); [meanwhileinmongolia.com](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/custom-mongolian-clothing/) (snippet only)

**Mongol Costumes Centre / "Монгол костюмс" төв: the state and ceremonial deel maker**
- Lonely Planet says it "designs and manufactures many of the fabulous dels worn during the Naadam opening ceremony and at other state events". Visitors can see deel styles and do a professional photo shoot in costume. — [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/mongolia/ulaanbaatar/attractions/mongol-costumes-centre/a/poi-sig/1454988/357066)
- Runs a Museum of Mongolian Costumes. About 60 of the company's roughly 400 designs are on display. — [Legend Tour](https://legendtour.mn/eng/mongolia/regions/ub-costumes-museum.shtml); [2012 blog](https://mongoliaeconomy.blogspot.com/2012/02/museum-of-mongolian-costumes.html)
- Co-organiser, with the UB Governor's Office and others, of the "Дээлтэй Монгол" national costume festival. The 2025 edition, the 19th, was held 8 July 2025 at Sükhbaatar Square. — [ikon.mn 2025](https://ikon.mn/n/3g5z); [ikon.mn 2024](https://ikon.mn/n/36td)

**Festival / award landscape (where "best deel maker" titles come from)**
- "Дээлтэй Монгол" names a best district, best organisation, **best deel craftsman (шилдэг дээл урлаач)**, best-dressed family, best foreign participant and best child. It also hosts a deel-makers' exhibition and sale. **The 2024–2026 winners' names were not found.** — [ikon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/3g5z); [ikon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/36td)
- **"Гоёл" (Goyol) is a fashion festival/competition, not a deel brand.** It is described as Mongolia's biggest fashion show. Its "best national costume design" award went to **O. Urantsetseg ("Эртний монгол хийц" / Ancient Mongol Craft)** in both 2017 and 2020. Grand Prix: 2014 to a designer from Ayas Cashmere, 2020 to N. Baljinnyam. — [ikon.mn Goyol 2014](https://ikon.mn/n/1tk); [ikon.mn 2017](https://ikon.mn/n/15x5); [ikon.mn 2019/20](https://ikon.mn/n/1qhx)

**Other national-clothing brands named in Mongolian media (less evidence of premium standing)**
- **Өв (Öv)**: national-clothing brand. Its Tsagaan Sar 2025 collection of men's and women's deels includes everyday and work deels, with a branch on the 4th floor of the State Department Store (Улаанбаатар Их Дэлгүүр). — [ikon.mn, Feb 2025](https://ikon.mn/n/3c57)
- **Mon Couture**: designer Kh. Batzaya. Deel-focused, with demand from Inner Mongolian buyers. — [news.mn](https://news.mn/r/2114869/) (via search summary)
- **Үжин Mongolia**: combines heritage and modern design. **Ялгуун (Yalguun)**: designer S. Uuganbat, makes wearable national-style pieces. **AtoZ**: designer L. Amarjargal. — [yalguun.mn](https://yalguun.mn/post/125930); [buro247.mn](https://www.buro247.mn/fashion/egelg-y-zagvaryg-eng-neer-b-teegch-l-amarzhargal.html); [business.mn 2020](https://business.mn/2020/04/10/%D2%AF%D0%BD%D0%B4%D1%8D%D1%81%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B9-%D1%85%D1%83%D0%B2%D1%86%D1%81%D1%8B%D0%B3-%D1%82%D1%80%D1%8D%D0%BD%D0%B4-%D0%B1%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%B6-%D0%B1%D1%83%D0%B9-%D0%BC%D0%BE)
- **"100" brand**: hand-painted silk shirts, deels and trousers in limited runs of 100, with designs varied per customer order. — [business.mn](https://business.mn/?p=36199)
- **Shilmel Torgo (Шилмэл торго)**: listed by Lonely Planet as one of several shops on a strip that make custom deels. This is the only "Torgo" match found. — [Lonely Planet mirror](https://www-lonelyplanet-com-6c06.imagizer.com/mongolia/ulaanbaatar/shopping/shilmel-torgo/a/poi-sho/1459830/357066)
- **Gumuda**: a concept boutique stocking many Mongolian designers, reportedly 20+ brands including Michel & Amazonka. — [Afar](https://www.afar.com/magazine/you-need-to-know-about-mongolias-fashion-scene)

**Candidates in the brief that did NOT check out**

- **Goyol**: a fashion festival (see above). One souvenir guide also lists "Goyol" outlets without detail. — same source

### Inferences
- For "premium/designer" deels, Michel & Amazonka is the clear headline brand, with the best international reputation and evidence of state-level clients. For classic, ceremonial, state-grade deels, Mongol Costumes is the clearest institutional choice.
- O. Urantsetseg / "Эртний монгол хийц" is a good lead for an award-winning traditional-craft deel maker: two Goyol national-costume awards. Its current trading status is unknown.
- The Mongolian deel market appears fragmented. An older gogo.mn-cited study estimated about 100 firms making traditional/modern clothing and souvenirs, about 10% of them luxury. — [mongolia.gogo.mn](https://mongolia.gogo.mn/r/3y243) (snippet only; date unknown)

### Gaps
- "Дээлтэй Монгол" best-deel-craftsman winners for 2023–2026 were not found in search results.
- No customer review data (Google/TripAdvisor ratings) could be found.
- No 2026-dated coverage of any deel brand was found. The newest items are from Feb 2025 (Өв) and July 2025 (festival).

## Price range, specialties, custom orders/appointments

### Takeaway
Only rough, mostly undated price benchmarks exist. Custom silk deels are widely available in UB. Expect a custom silk deel to cost roughly a few hundred thousand MNT and up, and designer pieces more.

### Cited Findings
- Shilmel Torgo: custom silk deel about **₮280,000** (Lonely Planet, undated). — [Lonely Planet mirror](https://www-lonelyplanet-com-6c06.imagizer.com/mongolia/ulaanbaatar/shopping/shilmel-torgo/a/poi-sho/1459830/357066)
- Silk fabric costs **₮8,000–120,000 per metre**. A women's deel needs about 5–6 m. — [meanwhileinmongolia.com](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/custom-mongolian-clothing/) (snippet)
- A tour operator's custom-deel experience costs **US$300/person, fabric excluded**, made in 3 days or less. — [meanwhileinmongolia.com](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/custom-mongolian-clothing/) (snippet)
- Another guide says custom deels take **14–21 days** depending on design complexity. — [selenatravel](https://www.selenatravel.com/blog/mongolian-traditional-clothing) (via search summary)
- Specialties:
  - Michel & Amazonka: modern, statement deels mixing traditional and European styles, plus bespoke ceremonial work (the Olympic pieces were made to measure).
  - Mongol Costumes: ceremonial, historical and state deels, plus costume photo shoots.
  - "100" brand: hand-painted silk, made per customer order.
  - Өв: everyday and work deels.
  - — sources as above

### Inferences
- Bespoke ordering appears standard among the deel makers listed. Michel & Amazonka's walk-in or appointment policy for custom deels is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- No 2026 price lists for Michel & Amazonka, Mongol Costumes, Өв or others.
- No wedding-deel specialists were identified.
- Appointment and booking procedures were not found for any brand.
