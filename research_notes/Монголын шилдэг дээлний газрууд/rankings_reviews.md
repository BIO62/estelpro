# Rankings, awards and customer reviews of Mongolian deel makers (as of Nov 2026)

Method note: research done via web search only. Direct page fetches failed for meanwhileinmongolia.com, growyourclothingbrand.com and wanderlog.com: these hosts are not in the session's network allowlist, so the claims below come from search-result summaries and were not read in full on the page. No Google Maps rating or review counts, and no Facebook follower counts, could be retrieved. Treat all contact details as needing verification.

## Which deel brands/tailors appear in "top/best" lists, awards or competitions (2022-2026)?

### Takeaway
No independent, authoritative "top 5 deel makers" ranking exists for 2022-2026. The main national competition, "Дээлтэй Монгол" наадам, does have a "Шилдэг дээл урлаач" (best deel maker) category, but searches found no published winner names. Taking the evidence together, five names recur most: **Michel & Amazonka** (strongest evidence: designed the 2024 Olympic uniforms and gets wide international press), **Tod Torgo** (custom tailor named "one of the best" in travel guides), **Mongol Costumes Centre** (makes Naadam-ceremony and state deels, and co-organises Дээлтэй Монгол), **TORGO** (luxury silk atelier running since 2000) and **Moncouture** (custom couture deels). Mogul Fashion, Өв, KADR, Urlakhui (Урлахуй) and Goyol Cashmere are secondary candidates.

### Cited Findings
**Competitions / awards**
- "Дээлтэй Монгол" наадам award categories include Шилдэг дүүрэг, Шилдэг аж ахуйн нэгж, **Шилдэг дээл урлаач**, Хамгийн сайхан дээлтэй гэр бүл/оролцогч/хүүхэд (2024 programme) — [tourism.ub.gov.mn](https://tourism.ub.gov.mn/?p=4965); [Mnb.mn 2024 programme](https://www.mnb.mn/i/313893)
- The festival is co-organised by Нийслэлийн Аялал жуулчлалын газар, Соёлын яам, Монголын Аялал жуулчлалын холбоо and the **"Монгол костюмс" төв** — [isee.mn](https://isee.mn/n/66374); [mto.mn](https://mto.mn/news/3)
- The 19th "Дээлтэй Монгол-2025" was held on 8 July 2025 at Сүхбаатарын талбай. It included a deel makers' exhibition and sale and named winners in categories such as the deel maker category — [mnb.mn](https://www.mnb.mn/i/331355); [ubn.mn](https://ubn.mn/p/86655); [mn.wikipedia](https://mn.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D1%8D%D1%8D%D0%BB%D1%82%D1%8D%D0%B9_%D0%9C%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%BB_%D0%9D%D0%B0%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B0%D0%BC_2025). **No winner names for the "Шилдэг дээл урлаач" category (2022-2025) were found.**
- 2022 results published by Khan-Uul district covered only individual participants (for example the best-dressed couple: Б.Мягмаржаргал and Н.Дөлгөөн), not makers — [khanuul.mn](https://khanuul.mn/2022/07/29/%D0%B4%D1%8D%D1%8D%D0%BB%D1%82%D1%8D%D0%B9-%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%BB-2022-%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B0%D0%BC%D0%B4-%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%BB%D1%86%D1%81%D0%BE%D0%BD-%D1%85%D0%B0%D0%BD/)
- Goyol-2025 (37th Goyol, Mongolia's largest fashion festival, held Nov 2024 at Shangri-La): Ө.Мөнхцэцэг, founder of the **"Урлахуй"** brand, won best designer, and Н.Галдамаа won best young designer. This is single-source (a search summary of the Өдрийн сонин page) and a second search could not confirm it. The award is a general fashion prize, not a deel prize — [unuudur.mn](https://www.unuudur.mn/a/272466); [ikon.mn Goyol coverage](https://ikon.mn/n/3ag9)
- GOBI JSC won the Best Fashion Design Award at "Mungun Mod 2025" (cashmere, not deel-specific) — [info.gobi.mn](https://info.gobi.mn/en/blogs/gobi-cashmeretech-silver-tree-2025-award_en)

**Michel & Amazonka**
- Founded in 2015 by sisters Michel and Amazonka Choigaalaa. It designed Mongolia's Paris 2024 Olympic uniforms, which drew wide international attention — [TIME](https://time.com/7003999/paris-olympics-uniforms-designers-mongolia/); [Highsnobiety](https://www.highsnobiety.com/p/mongolia-olympic-uniforms/); [Design Scene](https://www.designscene.net/2024/07/mongolia-olimpic24.html); [Mongolian Gallery](https://www.mongoliangallery.com/blogs/news/the-olympic-designs-that-sparked-a-cultural-renaissance-michelamazonka)
- Michel & Amazonka and KADR are said to regularly rework the deel silhouette for modern wear — [growyourclothingbrand.com "12 Mongolian Clothing Brands (2026)"](https://growyourclothingbrand.com/blog/clothing-brands-by-country/top-mongolian-clothing-brands-you-should-know/) (via search summary)
- Called the "hottest store in Ulaanbaatar for deels" — [Meanwhile in Mongolia, "Where to Buy a Mongolian Deel"](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/where-to-buy-mongolian-deel/) (via search summary)

**Tod Torgo (Тод торго)**
- Called "one of the best places in the city to get a deel made just for you". Floor-to-ceiling shelves of fabric, 100% silk and cotton, roughly one week for a custom deel — [Meanwhile in Mongolia](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/where-to-buy-mongolian-deel/); [Meanwhile in Mongolia custom deel](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/custom-mongolian-clothing/) (via search summaries)

**Mongol Costumes Centre (Монгол костюмс төв)**
- "Designs and manufactures many of the fabulous dels worn during the Naadam opening ceremony and at other state events." It has a small museum — [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/points-of-interest/mongol-costumes-centre/1454988)
- Co-organiser of Дээлтэй Монгол наадам — [isee.mn](https://isee.mn/n/66374)

**TORGO (luxury fashion house)**
- Began as a small Ulaanbaatar atelier in 2000 focused on bespoke silk tailoring. Its work has been shown in Paris, Singapore, Japan, Russia and China and featured on Mongolian postage stamps, and it made the costumes for the stage production "Mongol Khan" — [TORGO YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@torgo.official) (self-description); [Facebook: Crystal TORGO](https://www.facebook.com/TORGO.SILK/)

**Moncouture**
- Custom couture deels, described as "a splurge" for people who already have a design in mind, with 14-21 days for a custom order — [Meanwhile in Mongolia](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/where-to-buy-mongolian-deel/). Designer Х.Батзаяа (Batzaya.Kh), traditional collection in 2017 — [Pinterest](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/fashion-salon--436989970108071712/) (old)

**Others mentioned**
- Mogul Fashion: the travel guide's pick for distinctive patterned fabrics — [Meanwhile in Mongolia](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/where-to-buy-mongolian-deel/)
- "Өв" brand: national deel collection for Lunar New Year 2025, including men's silk and sheepskin-silk deels and women's hand-decorated silk deels. Source is a promotional post — [ikon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/3c57)
- Katya Zol is listed for "modernized deel practical for everyday wear" — [thetop10s.org](https://thetop10s.org/top-10-fashion-designers-in-mongolia/) (low-authority list site)
- Goyol Cashmere designer Baysa's team won the competition to design Team Mongolia's ceremonial outfits, which revive the deel silhouette. This conflicts with other sources that credit Michel & Amazonka; the two may have made different parts of the kit — [undergroundsound.eu](https://undergroundsound.eu/culture/exclusive-interview-with-mongolia-olympic-uniform-designer-from-goyol-cashmere/)
- Narantuul (Black Market): the cheapest, biggest selection of ready-made deels — [Meanwhile in Mongolia](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/black-market/); [Gogo Mongolia](https://mongolia.gogo.mn/r/momow)
- 5th floor of the State Department Store (Улаанбаатар их дэлгүүр): a ready-made deel and souvenir floor — [Meanwhile in Mongolia](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/where-to-buy-mongolian-deel/); [TripAdvisor review](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g293956-d7380243-r436261790-Ulaanbaatar_Department_Store-Ulaanbaatar.html)

### Inferences
- A defensible "top 5" built on independent evidence (awards, state commissions, press, travel-guide endorsements) is: 1) Michel & Amazonka, 2) Mongol Costumes Centre, 3) TORGO, 4) Tod Torgo, 5) Moncouture. Mogul Fashion and Өв are alternates. This is a synthesis, not a published ranking.
- English travel-blog coverage leans heavily on one source (Meanwhile in Mongolia), which also sells its own custom-deel tour (US$300 per person excluding fabric). That is a potential conflict of interest.
- Tod Torgo and TORGO appear to be different businesses: one is a custom tailor on Seoul St, the other a luxury atelier. Sources do not confirm whether they are related.

### Gaps
- Names of the "Шилдэг дээл урлаач" winners at Дээлтэй Монгол 2022-2025 were not found. The likely sources are the tourism.ub.gov.mn and Монгол костюмс Facebook pages, which could not be fetched.
- Deel-specific line-ups for Ulaanbaatar Fashion Week 2024-2026 were not found.
- No Mongolian-language "шилдэг дээлний брэнд" ranking article from ikon/gogo/news.mn/lemonpress/eguur was found.
- No Reddit r/mongolia threads surfaced.

## Google Maps ratings, review counts, Facebook followers, common praise and complaints

### Takeaway
Hard rating data is almost entirely missing: Google Maps and Wanderlog pages could not be read. Only fragments of qualitative review content were found.

### Cited Findings
- Mogul Fashion: one review (5/5, Feb 2026) on Wanderlog — [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/12224153/mogul-fashion)
- Michel & Amazonka (Galleria): Wanderlog shows only a brief 5-star snippet. No count was retrieved — [Wanderlog list](https://wanderlog.com/list/geoCategory/122492/the-coolest-clothing-stores-and-boutiques-in-ulaanbaatar)
- Mongol Costumes Museum, TripAdvisor: "costumes are beautiful, guide knowledgeable", but "very small, basically one room". Entry 3,000₮. Visitors can reserve a traditional outfit in their size to buy — [TripAdvisor r502662989](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g293956-d12289622-r502662989-Mongol_Costumes_Museum-Ulaanbaatar.html); [TripAdvisor r515909157](https://www.tripadvisor.com.ph/ShowUserReviews-g293956-d12289622-r515909157-Mongol_Costumes_Museum-Ulaanbaatar.html)
- Tumentorgo traditional clothes store: one reviewer compared its silk quality and prices unfavourably with another shop — [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/12458406/tumentorgo-mongolian-traditional-clothes-store)
- Narantuul: cheapest, but expect little bargaining room — [Meanwhile in Mongolia](https://meanwhileinmongolia.com/black-market/)
- Price benchmark: a Naadam deel costs 120-150k₮ (older article) — [ikon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/1m7t)

### Inferences
- Common praise across the custom makers: fabric choice and silk quality (Tod Torgo) and design innovation (Michel & Amazonka). Common complaints: custom work takes a long time (1-3 weeks) and costs more at boutique ateliers.
